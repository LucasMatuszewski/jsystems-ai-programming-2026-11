# Complaint Policy — Demo Electronics Store (synthetic example)

> **Synthetic document for the Hardware Service Decision Copilot PoC.** It describes a fictional online electronics store selling to consumers in Poland. It is loosely based on EU/Polish consumer law (the seller's liability for non-conformity of goods with the contract) but is **not legal advice** and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Replace it with the company's approved document before any production use.

The rules are numbered so the agent can cite them in its justification (for example "rule C-4").

## C-1 Scope

1. This policy covers complaints about goods that are defective or do not work as described (non-conformity with the contract), bought by consumers from the store.
2. A complaint is different from a return. If the customer simply does not want the goods, they should use the Return Policy.
3. **PoC assumption:** the date of purchase entered by the customer is treated as the date of delivery.

## C-2 Liability period

1. The store is liable for non-conformity that appears within **2 years** of delivery.
2. A complaint about goods delivered more than 2 years ago is **rejected**. The agent tells the customer that a manufacturer's warranty may still apply, if the manufacturer offers one.

## C-3 Presumption in favour of the customer

1. A non-conformity that appears within 2 years of delivery is presumed to have existed at delivery, unless the store proves otherwise.
2. Therefore the agent recommends **Reject** for condition reasons only when the photo shows **clear, specific evidence** that the customer caused the damage (see C-5). Suspicion alone leads to **Manual review**.

## C-4 Covered defects (examples)

These typically indicate a manufacturing or material defect and lead to **Accept**:

- dead or stuck pixels, lines or discolouration on a screen without any visible impact point;
- a battery that swells without any external damage to the housing;
- hinge, port or button failure without signs of force, impact or liquid;
- delamination, peeling or cracking of the housing or coating without impact marks;
- a device that does not power on, when the description is consistent and the photo shows no user-caused damage.

## C-5 Damage not covered (user-caused)

These are not covered by the store's liability and lead to **Reject** when the photo clearly shows them:

- a cracked or shattered screen or housing with a visible impact point (a "spider web" crack from one point, dents, chipped corners);
- liquid damage: corrosion, water marks, liquid under the screen;
- burns, melting or scorch marks;
- unauthorised modifications or visible opening of the device by a third party (missing screws, broken seals, non-original parts);
- damage from obvious misuse (bent chassis, crushed parts).

## C-6 Cases that need a person

The recommendation is **Manual review** when any of these is true:

- the defect cannot be seen in a photo (for example software errors, intermittent faults, battery life) and the evidence is only the customer's description;
- the photo is blurred, too dark, shows the wrong device or category, or does not show the reported area;
- the description and the photo contradict each other;
- both a covered defect (C-4) and user-caused damage (C-5) are visible.

## C-7 Remedies

1. If the complaint is accepted, the customer may first ask for **free repair or replacement**.
2. The customer may ask for a **price reduction or withdrawal from the contract** (refund) only when repair or replacement is impossible, the store refused it or failed to deliver it, the defect reappears, or the defect is significant.
3. The agent may suggest the likely remedy, but the final remedy is chosen by customer service together with the customer.

## C-8 Response time

The store responds to every complaint within **14 days** of receiving it (company commitment).

## C-9 Next steps for the customer (procedure)

1. Keep the proof of purchase.
2. Back up the data on the device, if possible, and remove any passwords or account locks.
3. Pack the device securely, with the accessories needed to show the defect (for example the charger for charging faults).
4. Send it to the store's service department (the address is given in the confirmation from customer service), or wait for a courier if customer service arranges one.
5. Customer service verifies the complaint and confirms the final decision by email.
