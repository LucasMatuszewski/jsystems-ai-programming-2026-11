# Return Policy — Demo Electronics Store (synthetic example)

> **Synthetic document for the Hardware Service Decision Copilot PoC.** It describes a fictional online electronics store selling to consumers in Poland. It is loosely based on EU/Polish consumer law but is **not legal advice** and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Replace it with the company's approved document before any production use.

The rules are numbered so the agent can cite them in its justification (for example "rule R-3").

## R-1 Scope

1. This policy covers returns (withdrawal from the purchase without giving a reason) of goods bought by consumers from the store's online shop.
2. A return is different from a complaint. If the customer reports a defect or damage that they did not cause, the customer should file a complaint instead (see the Complaint Policy).
3. **PoC assumption:** the date of purchase entered by the customer is treated as the date of delivery.

## R-2 Statutory withdrawal period (days 0–14)

1. The customer may return the goods within **14 days** of delivery without giving a reason.
2. During this period the customer may handle the goods only as they could in a physical shop: unpack them, inspect them and check that they work.
3. If the goods show signs of use beyond that (scratches, wear, dirt, damage), the return is **still allowed**. The store may deduct the loss in value from the refund. Only a service employee sets the deduction, after physical inspection.
4. The agent never rejects a return inside the 14-day period because of the condition of the goods. If the photo shows signs of use or damage, the recommendation is **Manual review**.

## R-3 Extended goodwill return period (days 15–30)

1. As a goodwill gesture, the store accepts returns from day 15 to day 30 after delivery, **only** when all of the following are true:
   - the goods show no visible signs of use (no scratches, scuffs, dents, fingerprint residue on matte surfaces, worn keys or buttons);
   - the goods have no damage;
   - the goods can be resold as new.
2. If the photo clearly shows signs of use or damage, the goodwill return is **rejected**.
3. If the photo does not allow the condition to be judged (blurred, too dark, device only partly visible), the recommendation is **Manual review**.

## R-4 After 30 days

1. Returns more than 30 days after delivery are **rejected**.
2. If the customer describes a defect, the agent informs them that they can file a complaint instead.

## R-5 Hygiene-sensitive goods

1. In-ear headphones and earbuds that came in sealed packaging and have been unsealed may be excluded from returns for hygiene reasons.
2. For the category "Headphones / Audio", if the photo or description indicates in-ear products used outside their sealed packaging, the recommendation is **Manual review**.

## R-6 Completeness

1. The customer should return the goods with all accessories received (charger, cables, manuals). The original packaging is required for goodwill returns (R-3) and recommended for statutory returns (R-2).
2. Missing accessories reported by the customer are not a reason to reject a statutory return (R-2), but they are noted for the service team.

## R-7 Refund

1. The store refunds the payment within **14 days** of receiving the customer's withdrawal statement. The store may withhold the refund until it receives the goods back.
2. The agent does not state refund amounts and does not promise a refund date beyond this rule.

## R-8 Next steps for the customer (procedure)

1. Pack the goods securely, with all accessories.
2. Send them to the store's returns department (the address is given in the confirmation from customer service).
3. Customer service verifies the request and confirms the final decision by email.
4. The return shipping cost is paid by the customer, unless customer service decides otherwise.
